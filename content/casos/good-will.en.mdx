When we can count on "Good Will" in our companies, there will be signals.

"Good Will" is nothing more than the good name or reputation that a person (natural or legal), a venue, a product, or a service has in the eyes of third parties. Of course, like everything in life, it is the result of years of good practices, CSR, innovation, leadership, sustainability, ethics, transparency, commitment, and — most importantly — integrity.

It is essential that the internal and external communications strategies of your company develop tactics that align its DNA and reality with the perception that all the parties involved (customers, suppliers, public opinion, employees, partners) have of it.

## Why is "Good Will" important?

- **Global impact:** large companies operate in multiple markets and cultures. A solid "Good Will" helps overcome cultural barriers and earn the trust of customers and employees worldwide.
- **Talent attraction:** the best minds want to work for companies with a good reputation. "Good Will" attracts valuable and committed talent.
- **Customer loyalty:** a solid "Good Will" fosters customer loyalty. Consumers buy from companies they trust and whose values they share.
- **Resilience in crisis:** in times of crisis, a solid "Good Will" acts as a protective buffer. It helps the company recover more quickly and with less damage to its reputation.

## How to build "Good Will" in your company?

- **Transparency and ethics:** be transparent in all your operations and act ethically in every decision. Honesty and integrity are fundamental to building "Good Will."
- **Social and environmental responsibility:** commit to sustainable and responsible business practices. Contributing positively to society and the environment generates a positive image.
- **Coherent communication:** your message must be coherent across all platforms and in all markets. Consistency strengthens trust.
- **Listen and adapt:** listen to your stakeholders, from customers to employees. Learn from their feedback and adapt your policies and practices accordingly.
- **Long-term commitment:** "Good Will" is built over time. Be patient and stay committed to your vision and values over the long term.

"Good Will" is an intangible asset of great value, so much so that it is taken into account in national and global commercial laws. Furthermore, your company's good name positions it advantageously against the competition.
